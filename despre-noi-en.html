Who we are

One team. Two areas of expertise.

Established in 1998, VIGILENT SECURITY SRL has strengthened its presence in the Romanian market through the continuous development of its operational capacity and the management of sites with diverse and complex security requirements. Together with VIGILENT SECURITY TEHNIC SRL, established in 2012, we provide clients with complementary services ranging from the organisation and provision of manned guarding to the implementation and maintenance of electronic security systems.

Our activity covers the entire security process: analysing protection needs, organising guarding arrangements, continuously monitoring alarm systems, responding rapidly to events, and designing, installing and maintaining video surveillance, intrusion alarm, access control, and fire detection and warning systems.

With the capacity to provide guarding services nationwide and a dedicated operational infrastructure for monitoring and intervention activities, we serve public institutions, businesses, industrial sites, infrastructure facilities and private properties.
