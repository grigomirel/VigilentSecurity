What it includes

Personal protection built around the actual risk.

Specialised personal protection — bodyguard service — is organised according to the individual profile, schedule, travel, public exposure and identified risks.

  • Protection of life and physical integrity
  • Protection against harassment punishable by law
  • Protection while the person is being transported
  • Reporting relevant incidents to the competent authorities

Definition based on Article 19(7) of Romanian Law no. 333/2003, consolidated version. View the official text.

Service organisation

Planned and coordinated protection.

The configuration is established after assessing the context and in accordance with the legal duties of bodyguard personnel.

Risk assessment

Identifying exposure, vulnerabilities and sensitive situations.

Protection plan

Defining measures, responsibilities and applicable procedures.

Close protection

Professional presence and a response adapted to the protected person.

Operational communication

Incident management

Applying procedures and notifying competent authorities when required.
