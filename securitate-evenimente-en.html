Control and coordination on site

Security for events.

We organise access control, surveillance and response for private, corporate and public events, adapted to the venue and participant flow.

About the service

Siguranță pentru participanți și organizatori.

The security arrangement is planned with the organiser, based on entrances, capacity, programme, restricted areas and possible incidents. Teams receive clear roles and communication procedures.

  • Participant access control
  • Entry and exit flow management
  • Restricted-area protection
  • Patrols across the venue
  • Incident prevention and management
  • Coordination with the organiser
