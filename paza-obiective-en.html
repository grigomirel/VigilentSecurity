Guarding for properties.

We organise guarding services for offices, commercial spaces, warehouses, industrial sites, institutions and properties, based on their real risks and flows.

About the service

Protecție construită în jurul obiectivului.

Posts, schedules, patrols and guard responsibilities are established after assessing the location. The arrangement can cover people and vehicle access, vulnerable areas, the perimeter and out-of-hours activity.

  • Fixed posts and patrols
  • People and vehicle access control
  • Perimeter and sensitive-area checks
  • Visitor and supplier records
  • Incident management according to instructions
  • Reporting to designated contacts
