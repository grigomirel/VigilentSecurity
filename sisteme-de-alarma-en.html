About the service

Protecție construită în jurul obiectivului.

The alarm system is configured according to access points, vulnerable areas, operating schedule and the way the space is used. Components are selected for reliable detection and simple day-to-day operation.

  • Intrusion detection
  • Door and window protection
  • Indoor and outdoor detection
  • Audible and visual warning
  • Connection to the monitoring centre
  • Remote management options
